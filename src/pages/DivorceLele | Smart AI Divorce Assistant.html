Common questions

Clear answers about privacy, legality, and how the assistant works.

Is my case data and evidence kept private?

Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption and do not train our AI models on your personal case data. Your evidence vault remains strictly confidential, and you can securely wipe your data from our servers at any time.

Will this replace my lawyer?

No. DivorceLele is designed to make you the most prepared client your lawyer has ever had. By organizing your own timelines, evidence, and financial disclosures, you drastically reduce the billable hours your lawyer spends on administrative work, saving you money for actual legal strategy.

Is the formatted evidence accepted in court?

Yes. The assistant structures digital logs, financial affidavits, and timelines strictly adhering to current legal formats, including Section 63 BSA criteria for electronic records.
